# Lite Music — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

Lite Music (`az.lite.music`) is developed by Ali Aghayev.

**Lite Music does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.**

- The app has **no internet permission**, so it cannot send anything off your device.
- There are no ads, analytics, crash reporters or third-party SDKs.
- Favorites, playlists and settings are stored only on your device. Playlists you export are saved as .m3u files in your Music folder.
- Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

## Permissions
- `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` — to find and play the music files on your device
- `FOREGROUND_SERVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, WAKE_LOCK` — to keep playing in the background and show playback controls

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
